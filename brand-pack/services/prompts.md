# Services — 6 направлений (иконки + фото-пары)

Иконки: см. `brand-pack/brand/prompts.md` пункт 4 (единый набор).
Фото: **nano_banana_pro**, 4:5, одна опорная фотография на каждое направление — для карточек услуг.

## 1. Диагностика
```
Cinematic close-up of a diagnostic scanner display glowing cold white/blue,
connected via cable to a dark car's OBD port under the dashboard, extremely
shallow depth of field, void black interior tones, warm fog highlight on the
connector, no visible brand logos on the scanner. Precise, clinical, technological
mood.
```

## 2. Техническое обслуживание
```
Macro shot of a mechanic's gloved hand precisely torquing a bolt on a dark
engine bay component, cold directional studio light, matte steel and graphite
tones, shallow depth of field, extreme precision and cleanliness, no clutter,
no visible tools branding.
```

## 3. Ремонт
```
Close-up of a disassembled brake caliper and rotor laid out with geometric
precision on a dark matte surface, cold top-down light, sharp shadows, matte
steel tones with a faint muted gold reflection on the caliper edge, engineering
catalog photography style.
```

## 4. Детейлинг
```
Macro shot of a microfiber cloth gliding across a dark glossy car panel, leaving
a sharp reflective streak of cold light, water beading nearby, extremely shallow
depth of field, void black and warm fog tones only, no visible chemical bottles
or brand labels.
```

## 5. Защита автомобиля
```
Macro shot of a paint protection film edge being applied to a dark front bumper,
cold light emphasizing the transparent film's edge line, matte steel background,
extreme precision, minimal color, no visible tools branding.
```

## 6. Сезонное обслуживание
```
Close-up of a tire tread pattern half-lit in cold light with a subtle frost/mist
texture on one side, void black background, sharp geometric grooves, minimal
palette, no snow clichés, no bright winter blue.
```

Избегать во всех: видимые логотипы инструментов/химии, яркий свет, беспорядок на фоне, люди без перчаток/неаккуратный вид.
