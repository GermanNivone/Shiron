# Cars — референс-модели как объекты дизайна

Модель: **nano_banana_pro**. Соотношение: 4:5 (портрет для карточек) и 16:9 (полноширинные).
Автомобили — объекты дизайна и инженерии, не "тачки": свет и линии важнее марки.

Референс-модели: Porsche 911, BMW M5, Mercedes-Benz S-Class, Range Rover, Audi RS6.

## Шаблон промпта (повторить для каждой модели)
```
Editorial automotive design photograph of a [MODEL], shot in a minimalist dark
studio pavilion, three-quarter angle emphasizing body line geometry, cold
directional light creating sharp highlight along the character line, deep void
black #0A0A0B background fading to graphite #1C1D20, polished dark floor with
faint reflection. Warm fog #C9C7C1 rim light on edges, no bright colors, no
racing livery, no visible license plate, clean unbranded wheels. Ultra sharp
detail, large format camera look, architectural stillness, treated as a design
object rather than a vehicle in use.
```

Замены [MODEL]:
1. `dark grey Porsche 911, low wide silhouette, rear three-quarter angle`
2. `matte graphite BMW M5, front three-quarter angle emphasizing the grille geometry`
3. `black Mercedes-Benz S-Class, side profile emphasizing the long roofline`
4. `dark green-black Range Rover, front angle emphasizing flat vertical surfaces`
5. `matte steel Audi RS6 Avant, rear three-quarter emphasizing quattro stance`

## Макро-вариант (деталь модели)
```
Macro detail shot of the [MODEL]'s front fender and headlight junction, cold
studio light, matte black and steel tones, extremely shallow depth of field,
single sharp focus line, no visible badge, architectural precision.
```

Избегать: яркие цвета кузова, видимые номерные знаки/шильдики конкретных салонов, гоночные ливреи, людей рядом с машиной, размытый/любительский свет.
