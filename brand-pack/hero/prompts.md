# Hero — главные полноэкранные кадры + motion

Фото: **nano_banana_pro**, 16:9 и 21:9 (широкий hero), высокое разрешение.
Motion: **seedance_2_0**, 16:9, 6-10 сек луп.

## Фото 1 — Hero статичный (главный экран)
```
Cinematic editorial automotive photograph, a dark matte car body (Porsche 911 or
Mercedes S-Class silhouette) parked inside a minimalist concrete-and-glass pavilion
at night, single directional cold light source raking across the body revealing
sharp reflections and deep shadows, polished concrete floor with soft mirror
reflection, void black #0A0A0B and graphite #1C1D20 ambient tones, warm fog #C9C7C1
highlight on the car's edge, one subtle muted gold #9C8352 glint on a trim line.
Wide negative space above the car for headline text overlay. Ultra-sharp macro
detail on the body panel, shallow depth of field toward background, architectural
photography style, Zeiss lens look, no people, no logos visible.
```

## Фото 2 — Hero альтернативный (крупный план, деталь)
```
Extreme close-up cinematic shot of a headlight and front fender crease of a
premium sedan, cold blue-white studio light, matte black and graphite tones,
water droplets on the surface catching light as tiny warm fog highlights, one
faint muted gold reflection streak. Extremely shallow depth of field, razor-sharp
focus line, dark negative space on one side for text, luxury automotive magazine
cover style.
```

## Motion 1 — Reveal фары в темноте (seedance_2_0)
```
Slow cinematic reveal: a car headlight in complete darkness gradually illuminates
from off to a cold precise beam, camera performs a slow parallax dolly-in, dust
particles drift through the light beam, matte black background, subtle muted
gold reflection on the lens rim. Extremely smooth slow motion, 24fps film look,
no camera shake, loopable start and end frame, 8 seconds.
```

## Motion 2 — Свет и капли на кузове
```
Macro cinematic shot of a dark car body panel, slow camera pan revealing shifting
cold light reflections sliding across the curved metal surface as if a light rig
moves overhead, a few water droplets roll slowly down the panel catching highlights.
Void black and matte steel tones only, no color grading shift, slow motion,
seamless loop, 6-8 seconds, architectural studio lighting.
```

## Motion 3 — Пыль/свет в павильоне (для фона секций)
```
Wide atmospheric shot inside a dark concrete pavilion, a single shaft of cold
light cuts diagonally through fine floating dust, slow almost imperceptible
camera drift, silhouette of a car parked in the background out of focus. Void
black ambient, extremely subtle, designed as a looping background layer behind
text, no visible motion artifacts, 10 second loop.
```

Избегать: смена цвета света на тёплый жёлтый, вспышки, резкие монтажные склейки, люди в кадре, читаемые номера/логотипы конкурентов.
