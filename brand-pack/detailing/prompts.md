# Detailing — крупные планы ухода за автомобилем

Модель: **nano_banana_pro**. Соотношение: 4:5 и 1:1 (для галереи/instagram-грид).

## 1. Полировка кузова
```
Macro shot of a polishing pad mid-motion on a dark glossy car hood, cold light
creating a sharp reflective arc, extremely shallow depth of field, void black
and warm fog tones, no visible brand labels on tools, precise circular motion
blur.
```

## 2. Чистка салона
```
Close-up of a soft brush cleaning stitched leather seat detail in a dark
interior, cold soft light from a window, matte steel and graphite tones,
extreme texture detail on the stitching, no clutter, calm precise mood.
```

## 3. Нанесение керамики/защитного покрытия
```
Macro shot of a ceramic coating applicator gliding across a dark panel,
leaving a sharp reflective sheen line, cold directional light, minimal palette,
a faint muted gold highlight on the reflection edge, extreme cleanliness.
```

## 4. Финальная инспекция
```
Close-up of a gloved hand inspecting a car panel under a raking cold light
(swirl-finder lamp style), the light revealing a perfect mirror-flat surface,
void black background, extreme precision, no visible imperfections, quiet
confident mood.
```

Избегать: пена/пузыри крупным планом (клише детейлинга), яркие цветные средства, бренды химии в кадре.
