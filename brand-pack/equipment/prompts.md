# Equipment — оборудование, инструменты, студия

Модель: **nano_banana_pro**. Соотношение: 4:5 и 16:9.
Индустриальный лофт, не "гараж". Никакого визуального шума.

## 1. Подъёмник

Wide architectural photograph of a minimalist car lift station inside a dark
concrete-and-glass workshop pavilion, a dark car raised on the lift, cold
directional light from above, deep shadows, void black and graphite tones,
extremely clean floor, no clutter, no visible brand signage, industrial-loft
precision rather than a typical garage.
```

```

Wide interior shot of a dark minimalist automotive workshop with polished
concrete floor, exposed steel beams, cold industrial lighting fixtures hanging
